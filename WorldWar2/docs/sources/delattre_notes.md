# Research notes: de Lattre, *The History of the French First Army* (1952)

**Citation.** Jean de Lattre de Tassigny, *The History of the French First Army*, translated by Malcolm Barnes, London: George Allen & Unwin, 1952 (English edition of *Histoire de la Première Armée française: Rhin et Danube*, Paris: Plon, 1949). Copy read: Internet Archive item `bwb_W7-ADK-972` (https://archive.org/details/bwb_W7-ADK-972), borrow-only (controlled digital lending); no public full text. Repository reference: **R686** (`data/references_meta.csv`, source family `delattre_french_first_army`).

**What the source is.** The memoir-history of the commander of Army B / French First Army, written with his staff from the army's records: one source family, independent of the US Army official histories (CMH) and of Wikipedia. Strong for French army, corps and division orders, dates and command arrangements; it is the commander's own account, so his view of disputes with Devers and SHAEF (Strasbourg, Stuttgart, Ulm) is partisan.

**Method.** Notes were taken in October 2026 from the borrowed reader's OCR text layer, one line per dated fact, in reading order. Format of each line: `date | unit | place/action | command relation | printed page | quote`. The page is the printed page number. The *quote* column is copied from the OCR text (often truncated, marked `...` or cut mid-word); the other columns are the note-taker's paraphrase. In the dataset only the quote and page of a line are cited as evidence; a fact found only in the paraphrase columns, or only in a truncated or garbled quote, is not used on its own. Obvious OCR misreadings are corrected in the dataset only where certain, and each correction is stated in the claim note (for example `rst`/`ist` = `1st`, `roth` = `10th`, `1gth` = `19th`, `Briangon` = `Briançon`, `manceuvre` = `manoeuvre`).

**Coverage and known gaps.**
- Covered: printed pp. 63-525 (Chapter IV to Chapter XVII), at a widening filter (Part Two densely; Parts Three to Five for dated formation facts).
- Not read: pp. 9-62 (prefaces, Chapters I-III, including Anvil-Dragoon planning, pp. 46-62) and the index (p. 526 onward).
- Gap: printed p. ~263 (reader leaf ~278): the text layer did not load after two attempts.
- Pages carrying only maps or plates have no text and no notes.
- The book was not re-opened when the notes were used, so a note cannot be checked beyond its own quote.

**Use in the dataset.** French follow-up (branch `french-followup-1944-45`, October 2026): confidence raises, new French claims at division level and above, and command-relation dates. See `docs/changelog.md` and `spot_check_french_followup.md`.
