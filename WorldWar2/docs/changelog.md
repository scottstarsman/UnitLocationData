Changes to the tables, schemas and scripts (not to individual claims). Newest first.

## review-items-1-7 (26 October 2026)

Items 1 to 7 of the review in [unit_references_review.md](unit_references_review.md) (the decisions listed there; item 8, the five place merges, is not part of this branch and stays open). One commit per item. Applied with `scripts/migrate_review_items.py` (run once per item; every edit asserts the old value).

### Item 1: the six former `attached:` entries

- **GER-FJR-006 with the 91st Luftlande Division (C0010) is now a relation, `CR0372`** (`attached`, from 6 June 1944, `end_unknown`). The source is the CMH volume Cross-Channel Attack (Harrison): ch. VII p. 237-238 says the 91st Division went into combat with an attached parachute regiment and p. 259-260 that Seventh Army ordered the division to take over command of the 6th Parachute Regiment on arrival; ch. VIII p. 292-293 has the regiment "under control of the 91st Division" on 6 June, and note 47 says the subordination ordered by Seventh Army was apparently not effected (von der Heydte directed the regiment without orders from the division). It is recorded as ordered, with that caveat in the relation's note; the start is the claim date because no source dates the order. References R447 and R448 (already in the table) are added to C0010 (`reference_ids`, `source`, `source_locator`, role `command_relationship` in `data/claim_reference_roles.csv`).
- **New evidence role `listed_by_higher_unit`** (vocabulary `relation_claim_role`, `data/command_relation_claims.csv`). A relation is evidenced by claims of its subject unit; the 6th Fallschirmjäger Regiment has no claim of its own, so the claim of the higher unit that lists it (C0010) is the evidence. Such a link is evidence only: it is not one of the listing claim's own relationships (the claim's rebuilt `command_relationships` does not change), the subject must be listed in the claim's `composing_units`, the link's `stated_note` keeps what the claim said, and a claim that lists a relation before it starts must state that start. `scripts/command_relations.py` and `scripts/validate.py` know the role.
- **The other five are not units of the table** (the 56th Infantry Brigade C0017, the 1st Canadian Parachute Battalion C0018, the 116th Infantry Regiment C0025, the 2nd and 5th Ranger Battalions C0031, the 2nd Irish Guards C0217; scope is division and above), so no unit and no relation was added. The word `(attached)` is removed from their `composing_text` entries and each relationship is kept, with the claim's date and source as stated, in the new [deferred_relations.md](deferred_relations.md) (rows D01 to D06). `scripts/validate.py` checks that file (claim exists and belongs to the claim unit, higher unit is a unit, relationship is in the vocabulary, the entry is still in the claim's `composing_text`).

### Item 2: the word "attached" is prohibited in the composition and engagement text

- **Rule.** `unit_refs.check` (run by `validate.py`) rejects the whole word `attach`, `attached`, `attaches`, `attaching`, `attachment` or `attachments` (any case, also after a hyphen) in `composing_text` (every entry and every qualifier in parentheses) and in `engaged_note`. The id columns carry no words. `detached` is a different word and is not caught. The former `attached:` prefix rule is covered by it.
- **17 claims converted** (the 12 plain entries of the review plus the 5 embedded ones; the 8 former `attached:` entries were done before and in item 1). Each claim's source was checked first. Recorded in `data/command_relations.csv` or as extra evidence of an existing relation (the claim of the higher unit, role `listed_by_higher_unit`, the claim's earlier wording kept in `stated_note`): C0105 (new relation, the 30th Division attached to XIX Corps from 13 June, CMH ch. IX p. 376-377, closed on 14 June by the division's next claim), C0089 (new relation, Kampfgruppe Heintz attached to the 17th SS Panzergrenadier Division, CMH ch. IX p. 378-379, start = claim date), and C0120 (CR0100), C0490 (CR0277), C0161 (CR0032), C0493 (CR0177), C0127 (CR0020), C0170 (CR0023), C0173 (CR0030) as additional evidence of relations that already existed (C0100, Combat Command A with the 101st Airborne Division, belongs to the 2nd Armored Division and not to the higher unit of CR0175, so it only loses the word). Not a unit of the table, moved to `docs/deferred_relations.md` (D07 to D11): C0506 (Combat Command B, 3rd Armored Division), C0507 (22nd Infantry Regiment), C0493 (the 22nd Infantry with Combat Command A), C0164 (C Squadron, 2nd County of London Yeomanry), C0211 (2nd Battalion, 915th Regiment, to the 916th Regiment, CMH ch. VIII). Relationship wording removed with no relation: C0037 (`GER-PZ-LEHRD (planned)`: the Panzer Lehr Division was only planned to join, CMH), C0030 (VII Corps with the 82nd and 101st Airborne Divisions; both divisions already have their relation with VII Corps), C0094 (4th Armoured Brigade, now `UK-ARM-004B (for Operation Epsom)`: the claim dates 13 June, the relation CR0059 starts 26 June, so the claim is not linked). C0094, C0161 and C0493 also gain an id in `composing_units` (UK-ARM-004B, GER-KG-WEIDINGER, US-CCA-002D); the five embedded ones get theirs in item 3.
- **Nothing else changed.** `who_controls` answers are the same on the 48-query sample; only the list of supporting claims of two lines grew (GER-KG-FREY gains C0170, US-CCA-002D gains C0493). The validator warnings are the same three.
- **Other words.** `notes` (140 claims `assign*`, 19 `operational control`/OPCON, 71 `attach*`, 7 placed/came under, 2 `subordinat*`, 1 under command), `current_activity`, `location_qualifier` and `strength_json` keep the word `attached` where it is the source's own description of a fact; they are free narrative and not parsed. Not banned: `composing_text` and `engaged_note` contain one `assigned` (C0052 `GER-SSPG-017D (assigned for blocking mission near Carentan)`, a task and not a command relation) and four `reinforced`. Recommendation: keep the ban to `composing_text` and `engaged_note` (the fields that were turned into ids), do not ban the narrative columns; the relationship words that matter are in the relations table.

### Item 3: the ten embedded unit ids

- **Ten entries in nine claims** (C0006, C0089, C0100, C0126, C0127 with two entries (Meyer and Boehm), C0145, C0170, C0173, C0184) had a unit id inside a free-text sentence ("battle group from the 1st SS Division: Kampfgruppe Frey (GER-KG-FREY; from 28 June)"). Each is now `<unit_id> (qualifier)` with the id in `composing_units`, like the other 25 qualified entries; the qualifier keeps the wording of the source minus the name that the id now supplies and minus the word "attached" (item 2). Nine ids are new in `composing_units` (GER-KG-MEYER twice, GER-KG-BOEHM, GER-KG-HEINTZ, GER-KG-ROHRBACH twice, GER-KG-FREY, GER-KG-WEIDINGER, GER-KG-VONLUCK; US-CCA-002D was already listed in C0100).
- **Validator.** An id inside an unqualified free-text entry of `composing_text` is now an error (it had to exist before): write `<unit_id> (qualifier)`.
- The relationships behind these entries are the relations of item 2 and the existing ones (see the evidence links of CR0020, CR0023, CR0030, CR0374).

### Item 4: "First Army" in C0039

- **Checked, left as text.** C0039 (21st Army Group, 7 June 1944) cites two pages about Montgomery's headquarters at Creullet (actu.fr, dday-overlord.com, read on 26 October 2026): neither mentions a First Army or any army (they give the dates of the visits, 7 to 22 June at Creullet and the move to Blay on 23 June). The claim's `composing_text` is `First Army` and the source does not decide which one. The US First Army was under the operational control of the 21st Army Group from 6 June (CR0166) and the First Canadian Army belonged to its order of battle (it was not yet in Normandy), so the name alone does not decide it; the evidence is not conclusive and the entry stays text (no id is guessed). If a source naming the army is found, replace it with the id (probably `US-ARMY-001A`) and add the claim to CR0166.

### Item 5: the 8th Armoured Brigade under the 50th Division (CR0061)

- **Sources checked** (read on 26 October 2026): Wikipedia, Gold Beach (the 50th Division order of battle lists the 8th Armoured Brigade under "Formations attached for assault phase"; the invasion section says the division was "supported by the 8th Armoured Brigade"); Wikipedia, 8th Armoured Brigade (it became an independent brigade in November 1942 and landed on 6 June supporting the 50th Division); British Military History, Independent Armoured Brigades, p. 7 (the brigade "landed in Normandy on 6 June 1944, supporting the 50th Infantry Division" and took part in the battle of "The Oden" (the Odon) from 25 June "under command of XXX Corps"); Pegasus Archive, XXX Corps (the brigade is listed as attached to the corps headquarters, with the 56th Infantry Brigade and elements of the 79th Armoured Division). None supports `organic`.
- **Fix.** CR0061 is `attached` (was `organic`), `end_unknown` true, start 6 June 1944 (the claim date, the landing; no source dates the end), with the three new references R512 (Gold Beach), R513 (British Military History PDF, p. 7) and R514 (Pegasus XXX Corps) as `command_relationship` evidence of C0020, a note stating the sources and the one disagreement (Pegasus puts the attachment at corps level; it is consistent with the corps holding the brigade and attaching it to the division for the assault). The 50th Division's claim C0017, which lists the brigade in `composing_units`, is added as evidence (`listed_by_higher_unit`). The standing parent is not named by a source, so there is an `unknown` placeholder for the brigade (CR0375, the same pattern as the 4th Armoured Brigade, CR0058) and the validator does not gain a warning. C0020's `parent_unit_id` (the 50th Division) is unchanged and its notes carry a review remark.
- **who_controls.** The 48-query sample has no query for this brigade, so its answers are unchanged there. The brigade itself changes: `who_controls.py UK-ARM-008B 1944-06-06` answered "no relationship interval" (organic is not an interval of the derived table) and now answers attached to the 50th Division, with the corps and army chain. The interval is open (no source dates the end; the same behaviour as the other open `attached` relations), so a later date such as 1 September 1944 gets the same answer: this follows the data, and a dated end would need a source.

## unit-ids-in-text-columns (27 October 2026)

Recommendation 3 (F3, unit references in text columns, the claim part) of [normalization_review.md](normalization_review.md). Applied with `scripts/migrate_unit_ids.py` (run once; `--verify HEAD` before the commit regenerates the old strings from the new data and diffs them). What was mapped and what is left for a decision: [unit_references_review.md](unit_references_review.md).

- **`engaged_with` is a `;` list of unit ids.** 157 claims have a value: 22 are `unknown` (unchanged, the only sentinel) and 135 name 177 opponents (58 distinct strings; the review's 60 counted two halves of one entry split at a `;` inside parentheses). 174 of 177 resolve to a unit id: 161 by display name, 8 by an alias (`Canadian 3rd Infantry Division`, `German 17th SS Panzergrenadier Division`, `German Nineteenth Army`) and 5 to the two new placeholders. 3 generic opponents ('British Royal Air Force', 'German Cherbourg naval forces (Admiral Hennecke)', 'British forces (Gold Beach sector; unit not named by CMH)') are in the new column `engaged_note`, verbatim; no id was invented for them.
- **List column, not a join table.** There is nothing to store per link beyond (claim, opponent), so `claim_engagements` would only repeat the claim file; a `;` list of ids matches `reference_ids` and keeps the claim files readable. The foreign key is declared in `claims.schema.json` (`separator`, `alsoAllowed` `unknown`) like `reference_ids`. Command relations stay a table because they have intervals and evidence.
- **Two placeholder units added** (`data/units.csv`, 354 to 356 rows): `GER-FJR-006` 6th Fallschirmjäger Regiment (regiment/battle_group, Luftwaffe; opponent in C0072, C0085, C0088) and `GER-SSHPZ-101` SS Heavy Panzer Battalion 101 (echelon `other`, Waffen-SS; opponent in C0096, C0097). Nationality Germany, theater western_europe, no parent, no formed date, a unit note saying why they exist.
- **`composing_units` is a `;` list of unit ids, `composing_text` is the new column for the rest.** 101 claims with a list (8 more are `unknown`, unchanged), 318 entries: 116 became ids (91 plain, 25 with a qualifier such as `(elements)` or `(from noon 28 July)` kept as `<unit_id> (<qualifier>)` in `composing_text`; 24 of those qualifiers were in the old text, one is the `(attached)` of C0010), 202 stay free text (regiments, battalions, companies and remarks that are not units; 10 embed a unit id; 'First Army' is ambiguous). 69 distinct units are now listed by id.
- **`attached:` forbidden.** Eight entries had the prefix (C0002, C0010, C0017, C0018, C0025, C0031, C0032, C0217). Two (C0002, C0032) are already relations in `data/command_relations.csv` (CR0001, CR0087) and became plain components; the other six (one unit with an id, five not units of the table) keep an `(attached)` qualifier and are listed for a decision. No relation was invented.
- **Validator.** Foreign keys for both id columns; no duplicate, no self, an opponent on the other side (`nationalities.alliance`); the free-text columns must not repeat the name of a unit that has an id, start with `attached:`, or contain an id that does not exist; qualifier entries need their id in `composing_units`; canonical `; ` joins. The old name-matching warning (`engaged_index`) is gone. Warnings 12 to 3: the 9 caused by unresolved opponents cleared (the other 3 are the `GER-COR-084C` list of more than three opponents and two 'attached entry without a standing parent' warnings, unchanged).
- **Scripts and outputs.** New `scripts/unit_refs.py` (list splitting, name resolution, the checks, the derived names); `common.load_all_claims()` derives `engaged_with_names` and `composing_units_names`; `build/all_claims.csv` carries both next to the id columns (54 columns, was 50: the two stored columns and the two derived ones; the old strings are regenerated by the derived columns). `build/coverage.csv` and `docs/coverage.md` count the two placeholders. `who_controls.py` gives identical answers on the 48-query sample. The historical migration scripts carry a NOTE.
- **Regeneration check.** The old `engaged_with` and `composing_units` strings are regenerated from the new data on the 157 and 109 non-blank cells (`unknown` included); 24 cells differ, all explained: variant spellings replaced by the display name (8 engaged: 3rd Canadian Infantry Division, 17th SS Panzergrenadier Division Götz von Berlichingen, Nineteenth Army (Germany)), the removed `attached:` prefix (8), short names replaced by unit names (6), 2 cells in another order. Every other column of the 566 claims and every old unit row is identical.
- **Not changed.** The 5 suggested place merges (still open in [places_review.md](places_review.md)), the branch/echelon split, commanders, `claim_evidence`, the lifecycle flags, the `units.csv` source columns (the third part of F3) and command relations.

## places-table (27 October 2026)

Recommendations 2 (F2, places) and the derived-value part of 6 (F6) of [normalization_review.md](normalization_review.md), on one branch. Applied with `scripts/migrate_places.py` (run once; `--verify HEAD` before the commit re-checks every old value against the new layout). The places that need a decision are in [places_review.md](places_review.md).

- **New table.** `data/places.csv` (383 places, `PL0001`...: 288 point, 79 area, 16 line; 14 aliases on 13 places; country FR 332, BE 28, NL 10, DE 5, GB 4, LU 4) with `schema/places.schema.json`. The 421 old `location_name` values collapsed to 383 places; 26 places absorb 2-5 names each, only where the folded first name part matched and the points were within 3 km. Places that merely share a geocoded point (a town and the sector or front built on it) stay separate: 38 shared-point groups (105 places) are listed for a decision.
- **Claim files.** `location_name` is replaced by `place_id` (foreign key), `location_qualifier` (the `vicinity` marker and the parenthetical claim context) and `coord_override`. Claim `latitude`/`longitude` stay; the validator requires them to equal the place's point, except for 20 claims whose own point differs by 37 m to 1.66 km and carries a reason in `coord_override`. Claim files: 559,083 to 521,464 bytes.
- **Coordinate provenance.** The `; coords: ...` tail is removed from all 566 `source` cells and now belongs to the place (`coord_reference_id` R001/R002, `coord_source_note`; four hand-worked points have a blank reference). The `match_pattern` column of `references_meta.csv` (it matched that tail) is removed.
- **Derived, no longer stored.** `location_temporal_offset_days` (0 of 354 differed), `strength_status` (the destroyed flag, else the new `strength_json.status`, else `unknown`; regenerated values equal the old ones on all 566 rows) and the 561 mechanical `location` roles of the coordinate services (`claim_reference_roles.csv` 619 to 58 rows; `build_references.derive()` rebuilds them, `build/claim_references.csv` unchanged). Of the 18 `strength_status` rows with no `strength_json` figures, 13 stay `unknown`, 2 follow from `destroyed`, and 3 became status-only `strength_json` records quoting their own notes (C0132, C0127 heavily_depleted; C0189 reduced). The three lifecycle flags are kept (not derivable).
- **Validator and checks.** New place checks (unique ids and names, sorted, vocabularies `place_kind` and `country`, aliases, coordinate reference is a geocoder and is cited by a claim at the place, every claim point equals its place's point unless overridden, override within 5 km), derived roles must not be stored, `strength_json.status` vocabulary. The independence count for confidence 0.9 ignores `gazetteer_geocoder` references (0 warnings before and after; 391 claims rest on one non-geocoder family, information only). `check_coordinates.py` checks claim/place consistency, warns for a point in the open Atlantic and, with `--duplicates`, lists points within 1 km.
- **Scripts.** `common.load_all_claims()` derives `location_name`, the offset, `strength_status` and the place columns; `build.py` writes `build/places.csv` (with `claim_count`) and `build/places.geojson` and adds `place_id`, `place_name`, `country`, `region`, `coord_reference_id`, `coord_source_note` to `build/all_claims.csv`. The historical migration scripts carry a NOTE.
- **Derived outputs.** `build/all_claims.csv` differs only in the new columns, `source` (coordinate tail removed, 566), `location_name` (27 rows, spelling normalised among merged names) and `strength_json` (10 rows, `status` added). `build/claims.geojson`: positions identical; properties add `place_id` and change `location_name` on the same 27 rows. `who_controls.py` gives identical answers on the 48-query sample; both audits and the validator warnings (12) are unchanged.
- **Not changed.** `engaged_with` / `composing_units` ids, the branch/echelon split, commanders, `claim_evidence`, and command relations beyond keeping them working.

## command-relations-table (26 October 2026)

Recommendation 1 of [normalization_review.md](normalization_review.md): command relationships become a table. Applied with `scripts/migrate_command_relations.py` (run once; `--verify 54933a8` re-checks the migration against the claim files of the commit before it).

- **New tables.** `data/command_relations.csv` (master: 371 relation rows, `CR0001`...; 348 distinct subject/higher/relationship triples, the rest are further intervals of the same triple, placeholders and entries known only from alternate or disputed claims), `data/command_relation_claims.csv` (evidence: 1,076 links, with `seq`, `role` and the `stated_*` dates each claim gave) and `data/command_relation_overlaps.csv` (the 19 contradictory overlaps, documented). Fields, keys and foreign keys are in `schema/relations.schema.json`; the new vocabularies `relation_start_basis`, `relation_end_basis`, `relation_claim_role` and `relation_overlap_kind` are in `schema/vocabularies.json`.
- **Claim files.** The `command_relationships` column is removed from all 114 claim files (776,126 to 559,083 bytes); `parent_unit_id` stays and is validated against the claim's relations. `common.load_all_claims()` rebuilds the text for the scripts and `build/all_claims.csv` carries it as a derived column in its old position, so the combined file has the same 42 columns. The rebuilt text equals the old one in meaning for all 566 claims (450 identical, 116 differ only in key order inside the entries: the order is now canonical).
- **Absorbed.** 31 claim entries with no named higher unit (the former `unit_id` null entries and the `unknown` sentinels) are 21 explicit `unknown` placeholder relations (blank `higher_unit_id`, note kept). 53 temporary relations (attached 37, operational_control 11, reserve 3, administrative_control 2) whose end no source dates have `end_unknown` true (38 with an end inferred from the next claim, 15 still open at the unit's last claim). The five undated German parent changes of June 1944 (21st Panzer, 12th SS Panzer, Panzer Lehr, I SS Panzer Corps, II SS Panzer Corps) are flagged `change_undated` (10 relation rows); no date was moved or invented.
- **Scripts.** `build.py` derives `build/command_relations.csv` from the master (all nationalities and dates, placeholders included: 356 rows instead of 315; the 315 old rows are identical), `who_controls.py` reads it (identical answers on every query that had one; a placeholder or a change-undated relation is now reported), `validate.py` checks the three tables (header, values, keys, foreign keys, roles against the stated dates, the interval rules, the evidence against the master, every contradictory overlap documented or an error) and validates the rebuilt per-claim entries as before, `audit_temporal_relationships.py` has three checks more (`master_overlap`, `end_unknown`, `change_flagged`). New library `scripts/command_relations.py`. The historical migration scripts that read the column carry a note.
- **Derived outputs.** `build/all_claims.csv`: only `command_relationships` changes (116 rows, key order). `build/command_relations.csv`: new `relation_id`, `end_unknown`, `change_undated` columns, 41 more rows (20 named relations outside the old window or nations, and the 21 placeholders). All other generated files are byte-identical.
- **Not changed.** The places table, `engaged_with` / `composing_units` ids, the branch/echelon split and the derived columns repeated from `units.csv` are untouched (they depend on this step only through `parent_unit_id`).

## normalize-cheap-fixes (26 October 2026)

Follows the review in [normalization_review.md](normalization_review.md) (items F1 small step, and the "cheap fixes" of its recommendation 4). Applied with `scripts/migrate_normalize.py`.

- **Command relationships.** The redundant `name` is dropped from all 1,061 `command_relationships` entries (the name comes from `units.csv`). The 16 entries with `unit_id` null (formations that are not units: "standing parent not identified" 14, "Army not shown in CMH table" 2) keep their wording as `note`; `unit_id` null is now only allowed with relationship `unknown` and a note. The `unknown` sentinel is gone from `parent_unit_id` (15 claims, now blank) and from `command_relationships` (the same 15 claims, now one entry `{"unit_id": null, "relationship": "unknown", "note": "parent not verified"}`).
- **units.csv.** The column `parent_only` (identical to `record_status = placeholder` on 354 of 354 rows) is removed; 16 columns remain.
- **Nationalities.** New `data/nationalities.csv` (10 rows: code, name, adjective, alt_adjectives, alliance, kind, note). Units, claims and operations refer to its `name`; the validator checks the references and that each unit id starts with the nation's code. The 3 `Allied` units keep the explicit multinational value. `operations.nationality_scope` `German` (2 rows) is now `Germany`.
- **Single-source vocabularies.** Echelon rank and display order, relationship groups (standing, active), `higher_than_organic` and `source_type` are new entries of `schema/vocabularies.json`; `validate.py`, `build.py`, `audit_temporal_relationships.py` and `who_controls.py` read them through `scripts/common.py`. `operations.py` and `build_references.py` read their vocabularies from the same file; the `nationality` and `operation_scope` entries were removed (the table replaces them).
- **Foreign keys.** `foreignKeys` in `claims.schema.json` and `units.schema.json`, the new `relations.schema.json` for the side tables, enforced by `validate.py`; `theater` is now one of the repeated unit columns checked on every claim.
- **Derived outputs.** `build/all_claims.csv`: columns `command_relationships` (all 566 rows) and `parent_unit_id` (15 rows) changed. `build/command_relations.csv`: `higher_name` normalised to `<adjective> <unit_name>` in 64 of 315 rows (it was inconsistent between claims, for example "First Army" and "US First Army"); no other column changed. `build/operations.csv`: `nationality_scope` in 2 rows. All other generated files are byte-identical.
