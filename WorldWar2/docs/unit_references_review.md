## `attached:` entries

Eight entries had the prefix `attached:` (in these old strings the prefix marks the first entry after it; the entries after it in the same list carry no marker). The prefix is gone and is now a validation error. A relationship belongs in `data/command_relations.csv`: where a relation with the attached unit as subject and the claiming unit as higher, valid on the claim date, already exists, the entry is just the component and nothing else is kept; where there is none the entry keeps the qualifier `(attached)` (the way `30th Infantry Division (attached)` was already written) so the claim loses nothing. No relation was added: an interval needs a unit as subject and a sourced date, and none of the eight entries has both a table unit that is not already covered and a date from a source.

## Decisions for the owner

Nothing below was changed in the data; each is a question the migration could not answer from the claims.

1. **`attached` relationships not in `data/command_relations.csv` (6 of the 8 old `attached:` entries).** One has a unit id: the 6th Fallschirmjäger Regiment with the 91st Luftlande Division on 6 June (C0010; unit `GER-FJR-006`, new placeholder). It is kept as `GER-FJR-006 (attached)`; no interval was added because the claim gives no dates or source for the attachment. Five are not units of the table, so no relation can name them as subject: the 56th Infantry Brigade (C0017), the 1st Canadian Parachute Battalion (C0018), 2nd and 5th Ranger Battalions (C0031), the 116th Infantry Regiment (C0025) and the 2nd Irish Guards (C0217, "from 16 September"); each is kept as text with `(attached)`. To get relations for them, add the unit as a placeholder and give a sourced date; say which. **Status: done on branch review-items-1-7 (item 1 commit). The 6th Fallschirmjäger Regiment with the 91st Luftlande Division is relation CR0372 (CMH Cross-Channel Attack ch. VII and VIII, with the caveat that the ordered subordination was apparently not effected on 6 June); the other five are not units of the table and are kept in docs/deferred_relations.md (D01 to D06). See changelog.**
2. **Other `attached` wording left as written.** The prefix was the only form forbidden. Seventeen more entries use the word in a qualifier or in text: `US-INF-030D (attached)` (C0105), `US-ABN-101D (attached)` (C0120), `US-INF-035D (attached)` (C0490), `GER-PZ-LEHRD (attached, planned)` (C0037), `US-COR-007C (82nd and 101st Airborne attached to VII Corps)` (C0030), "Combat Command B, 3rd Armored Division (attached)" (C0506), "22nd Infantry Regiment, 4th Infantry Division (attached)" (C0507), "(4th Armoured Brigade attached for Epsom)" (C0094), "(C Sqn 2nd County of London Yeomanry attached)" (C0164), "(2nd SS Kampfgruppe Weidinger attached 29 June)" (C0161), "Combat Command A, 2nd Armored Division, with the 22nd Infantry attached (temporary, from noon 28 July)" (C0493), "its 2nd Bn was split off at midday and attached to the 916th Regiment" (C0211) and the five (C0089, C0100, C0127, C0170, C0173) that embed a unit id (item 3). Whether to forbid the word everywhere, and to move each into `data/command_relations.csv`, is open. **Status: done on branch review-items-1-7 (item 2 commit): the word is rejected by the validator in composing_text (entries and qualifiers) and engaged_note; the 17 claims are converted (relations CR0373 and CR0374 new, seven existing relations gain the higher unit's claim as evidence, five relationships deferred as D07 to D11, three wordings only reworded); counts of the other relationship words are in the changelog.**
3. **Ten entries that contain a unit id inside a longer description** (the list is above, "embedded") stay text: they name a battle group of the table (`GER-KG-MEYER`, `GER-KG-ROHRBACH`, `GER-KG-FREY`, `GER-KG-HEINTZ`, `GER-KG-WEIDINGER`, `GER-KG-BOEHM`, `GER-KG-VONLUCK`, `US-CCA-002D`) but with a relation in the words ("attached battle group from the 275th Division"). The validator checks that every embedded id exists. They could become `composing_units` ids with the wording as qualifier once the relations they describe are in `data/command_relations.csv`. **Status: done on branch review-items-1-7 (item 3 commit): all ten embedded entries are now <unit_id> (qualifier) entries with the id in composing_units; an id inside unqualified free text is now a validator error.**
4. **"First Army" (C0039, 21st Army Group, 7 June 1944).** The name belongs to more than one unit of the table (the US First Army, the German First Army; the First Canadian Army is called "First Canadian Army"), and the claim does not say which army is meant. Kept as text, not guessed.
5. **8th Armoured Brigade under the 50th Division (C0017).** The old composing list put `attached: 56th Infantry Brigade; 8th Armoured Brigade` (the second entry without a marker), while `data/command_relations.csv` has the 8th Armoured Brigade as `organic` to the 50th Division (CR0061, 6 June 1944). The relation row was not changed (out of scope); 
6. **Battalion echelon.** `SS Heavy Panzer Battalion 101` has echelon `other` and no echelon letter in its id (`GER-SSHPZ-101`), and the 6th Fallschirmjäger Regiment has `regiment/battle_group` and `GER-FJR-006`; the echelon vocabulary has no battalion value and the id convention has no letter for a regiment or battalion. A `battalion` echelon would be a vocabulary change that touches no data today.
7. **Regenerated names differ from the old text in 24 cells (see `scripts/migrate_unit_ids.py --verify HEAD` in the changelog entry).** Wording changes are the display name replacing a short or variant form (Combat Command A, 17th SS Panzergrenadier Division, Nineteenth Army, 3rd Canadian Infantry Division) and the removed `attached:` prefix; two cells list the same entries in another order.
8. **The five suggested place merges** (Caumont, Mantes, Brest/Recouvrance, Omaha Beach, Hill 112) are still open in `docs/places_review.md`; this work did not touch places.
