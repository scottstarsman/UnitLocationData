# Coverage report and review queue

Generated by `python scripts/build.py` (through `scripts/coverage.py`) from `data/units.csv`, `data/claims/` and `data/references_meta.csv`. Derived outputs only: nothing here changes a claim. (The older `docs/coverage.md` is also generated and counts units and rows by nationality and echelon; this page explains the three tables below. A short reading of the current numbers is in [coverage_summary.md](coverage_summary.md).)

Definitions used throughout:
- **Claim:** every row of the claim files, whatever its `claim_status`.
- **Active span:** from a unit's first to its last dated claim. Months and gaps are counted inside it only; a unit that leaves the theatre is not "missing" after its last claim.
- **Source family:** the `source_family` of each reference in `reference_ids`, read from `data/references_meta.csv`, leaving out gazetteer and geocoder references (they place a point, not the unit).
- **Map-only row:** a row whose only source family is `loc_12ag_situation_maps` (the 12th Army Group situation maps). Scott treats these as coarse filler (methodology).

## build/coverage_by_unit.csv

One row per unit with at least one claim.

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `unit_id`, `unit_name`, `nationality`, `echelon`, `branch` | from `data/units.csv` (`echelon` is the unit type) |
| `first_claim_date`, `last_claim_date`, `span_days` | the active span |
| `claim_count` | claim rows |
| `conf_0_9`, `conf_0_7`, `conf_0_5`, `conf_0_3_or_less` | rows by confidence band (≥0.9, 0.7–0.89, 0.5–0.69, below 0.5) |
| `map_only_rows`, `map_only_share` | map-only rows and their share of `claim_count` |
| `source_families`, `source_family_list` | distinct source families over all the unit's rows (`;`-separated) |
| `longest_gap_days`, `longest_gap_from`, `longest_gap_to` | the longest stretch between two consecutive claim dates, and its end dates |
| `empty_months`, `empty_month_list` | calendar months inside the active span with no claim |

## build/coverage_by_month.csv

One row per nationality and month (months from the first to the last claim date of the whole dataset; months in which no unit of that nationality is active are left out).

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `units_active` | units of that nationality whose active span covers the month |
| `units_with_claim`, `units_without_claim` | of those, how many have at least one claim dated in the month, and how many not |
| `claim_rows`, `map_only_rows`, `map_only_share` | claim rows dated in the month, and the map-only share (blank when no rows) |

## build/review_queue.csv

Every unit with claims, ranked by where reading another source would help most. Each factor is scaled to 0–1 and weighted:

| factor | weight | scale |
|---|---:|---|
| `gap_score` | 0.30 | longest gap in days / 90, capped at 1 |
| `map_score` | 0.25 | map-only share |
| `low_conf_score` | 0.15 | share of rows below confidence 0.5 |
| `single_family_score` | 0.15 | 1 if all rows come from one source family, else 0 |
| `empty_month_score` | 0.15 | empty months / months in the active span |

`score` is the weighted sum (0–1); ties go by `unit_id`. `read_from` and `read_to` give the period to read: the longest gap when it is 30 days or more, else the whole active span. `reason` lists the weak points in words. `suggested_sources` is a fixed hint by nationality: FMS manuscripts and Lexikon/Tessin for German units, the CMH Green Books and combat chronicles for US, Ellis and Joslen for British, Stacey for Canadian, de Lattre and the French official histories for French units, and the official histories of the army a unit served under otherwise. It is a starting point, not a check that the source covers the unit.

Limits: the score does not know which sources exist for a unit; a unit with a single claim has no gap and scores low even though it is thinly covered (see `claim_count`); the weights are a first proposal and can be changed in `scripts/coverage.py` (`WEIGHTS`).
