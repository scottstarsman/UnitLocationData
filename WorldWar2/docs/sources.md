# Sources

Every claim row cites its own sources in the `source` column. This page lists what the proof of concept used, how reliable each source family is for this purpose, and licensing notes. **The complete list of references, one row per distinct source with its link, type, licence, claim count and link status, is [`data/references.csv`](../data/references.csv)** (generated by `scripts/build_references.py` from the claims and the hand-curated `data/references_meta.csv`; each claim lists its references in `reference_ids` (with `source_locator` where a page is known), `build/claim_references.csv` and `build/unit_references.csv` link each claim and unit to its references, and `source_family` groups references that are not independent: see `docs/methodology.md`). The table below describes the source *families*. It is not legal advice; the project owner should review it before publishing.

## Reliability notes

- Prefer a primary or semi-primary source (official histories, unit war diaries, regimental histories) over Wikipedia whenever one exists; Wikipedia is a finding aid.
- Where CMH and Wikipedia disagree on a date or place, both claims are recorded as a conflict group and the preferred row is the better-supported one (usually CMH).
- German strength figures come from secondary sources (Wikipedia pages quoting unit reports; CMH), not from the primary German returns. Treat `strength_json` as indicative.
- A second spot check of 15 Batch 1 rows (25 October 2026) led to the command-relationship and location-date changes described in `docs/methodology.md` and [`review/review_changes.md`](review/review_changes.md); the reviewer's citations were each re-checked and two of its claims could not be confirmed (the 704th Tank Destroyer Battalion landing on 12 July, and the Army "Essential to Success" page, which could not be opened).
- A reviewer's spot check of 15 rows led to five corrections (C0001, C0057, C0084, C0141, C0184); each was checked against the sources above and only changed where they support it. Rows whose confidence changed were re-scored by the rubric in `methodology.md`.
- Source conflicts recorded in `notes`: 90th Division commander change (CMH 13 June vs Wikipedia July), Feuchtinger and Choltitz ranks, 12th SS commander's rank, and several strength figures (21st Panzer 146 vs 112 tanks; 716th Division 7,000 vs 7,771 vs about 9,343 men).
