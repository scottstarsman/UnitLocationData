# Coverage

Generated by `python scripts/build.py` from `data/units.csv` and `data/claims/`. Do not edit by hand. Per-unit detail is in `build/coverage.csv`.

A **placeholder** is a unit that exists in the table (identity, echelon, sometimes a formation date) but has no claim file: it says nothing about where the unit was. **active** units have a claim file; **partial** is reserved for claim files known to be incomplete. See "Placeholder units" in `docs/methodology.md`.
